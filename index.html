Clip Studio BR

Clip Studio BR is a small content-production tool used by its owner to edit short vertical videos and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram and YouTube). It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other users.

What the app does

  • Cuts and formats videos into vertical (9:16) clips, with captions and credits.
  • Lets the owner review and approve each video before it is published.
  • Publishes approved videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.
