Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

Clip Studio BR is a private tool operated by a single individual (the "Owner") to publish video content to the Owner's own accounts. It has no public users.

2. Data we handle

  • Account authorization data: when the Owner connects their own TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic account identifiers through TikTok's official login and API. This is used only to publish videos the Owner has approved.
  • Content: videos, captions and credits created by the Owner, stored locally on the Owner's computer.

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.

3. How data is used and stored

Tokens and keys are stored only on the Owner's own computer, in a private configuration file, and are never shared, sold or used for advertising or profiling. Data is not sent to any party other than the platform (TikTok, Meta or Google) through whose official API a video is published.

4. Retention and deletion

The Owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok settings (Security and permissions) and delete the stored token. When access is revoked, the app can no longer act on the account.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from them.

Política de Privacidade (Português)

O Clip Studio BR é uma ferramenta privada, operada por uma única pessoa (o "Proprietário"), para publicar vídeos nas próprias contas do Proprietário. Quando o Proprietário conecta sua conta do TikTok, o app recebe um token de acesso e identificadores básicos pela API oficial, usados somente para publicar vídeos aprovados pelo Proprietário. Tokens e chaves ficam apenas no computador do Proprietário, não são compartilhados, vendidos nem usados para publicidade. O app não coleta dados pessoais de terceiros. O acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento nas configurações do TikTok.
